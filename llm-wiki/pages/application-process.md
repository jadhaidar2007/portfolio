# Steps

1. Register the company in the submission portal.
2. Submit a 2-page project sketch. The office answers within 4 weeks.
3. If invited, submit the full proposal with a cost plan and work plan.
4. An external expert panel scores technical novelty, market potential and team.
5. Decision letter within 10 weeks of the full proposal.

# Key facts

- **Application deadline:** 31 March 2027
- **Proposal language:** English or German

# Common rejection reasons

Missing cost plan, projects that are pure research without a pilot, and applicants
that already started the project before submitting. See [[eligibility]].
