# Sampling

Finance control audits a random 15% of funded projects each year, plus every project
with eligible costs above €120,000.

# What auditors check

- Timesheets match payroll for every claimed staff hour.
- External R&D invoices were procured with at least 3 quotes above €10,000.
- No double funding from another public programme.

# Key facts

- **Audit sample rate:** 15% of projects per year
- **Repayment on finding:** ineligible amounts plus 5% interest per year
