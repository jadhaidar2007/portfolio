# Who can apply

Companies with fewer than 250 employees and an annual turnover below €50 million.
The company must have a permanent establishment in the funding region. Universities
may join as partners but cannot lead a project.

# How much funding

- **Maximum grant:** €150,000
- **Funding rate small companies:** 50% of eligible costs
- **Funding rate medium companies:** 40% of eligible costs
- **Project duration:** 6 to 24 months

Eligible costs are staff costs, external R&D services, and prototype materials.
Travel and general overheads are not eligible. See [[reporting-obligations]] for how
costs must be documented.
