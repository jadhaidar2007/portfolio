# What the programme funds

The Nordlicht Innovation Grant (NIG) is a fictional public funding programme used as demo data.
It co-finances applied R&D projects by small and medium-sized companies that move a
technology from prototype to first industrial pilot.

Typical projects: sensor and chip prototypes, industrial automation, energy-efficiency
retrofits and AI tools used in production.

# Key facts

- **Programme owner:** Program Office North
- **Funding type:** Non-repayable grant
- **Application deadline:** 15 March 2027
