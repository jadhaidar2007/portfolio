# Interim reports

Funded companies submit a technical and financial interim report every 6 months.
The financial report lists staff hours per person per month, supported by timesheets.

# Final report

Due within 3 months after the project ends. It must show whether the pilot milestone
was reached. Missing the deadline pauses the final payment.

# Key facts

- **Interim report frequency:** every 6 months
- **Final report deadline:** 3 months after project end
- **Document retention:** 10 years

Audits follow the checklist in [[audit-checklist]].
