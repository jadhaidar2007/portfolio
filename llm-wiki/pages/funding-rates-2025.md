# Funding amounts in the 2025 call

- **Maximum grant:** €120,000
- **Funding rate small companies:** 45% of eligible costs
- **Funding rate medium companies:** 35% of eligible costs

Kept for projects approved under the 2025 call, which keep these rates until they end.
